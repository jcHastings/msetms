SMS Privacy Policy

Effective August 24, 2026 · Last updated August 24, 2026

This SMS Privacy Policy explains how M&S Loads LLC (“M&S Loads,” “we,” “us”) handles mobile telephone numbers and related information used for operational text messages sent through MS Express TMS. These texts are work dispatch messages to hired drivers and owner-operators. They are not marketing.

Who we are

M&S Loads LLC operates MS Express TMS, a transportation management system used to assign loads and send pickup, delivery, reefer, and status updates to drivers who work for or with us.

Mobile numbers we collect

We collect a driver’s mobile number when it is given at hire or when the driver is added in MS Express TMS. We use that number only to send operational load-dispatch texts and related support replies (for example, HELP).

We do not share or sell mobile numbers

We do not share or sell mobile telephone numbers. Mobile information and SMS opt-in or consent data will not be shared or sold to third parties, affiliates, lead generators, or anyone else for marketing or promotional purposes at any time. Carriers and our texting provider may carry the message as part of delivering it. That is not a sale or marketing share of the number.

How we use SMS information

We use mobile numbers and related dispatch details to:

  • Send operational load-dispatch texts to hired drivers and owner-operators
  • Tell a driver about pickup, delivery, reefer setpoint, and other load updates
  • Answer HELP requests and honor STOP opt-out requests
  • Keep an internal record of who was notified about a load

We do not use these numbers to send advertising, promotions, or sales texts.

Message types and frequency

Texts are operational load dispatch. They may include pickup and delivery details, appointment times, reefer setpoint, and other updates about an assigned load.

Message frequency varies with assigned loads. A driver may receive no texts on some days and several texts on a busy dispatch day. There is no fixed monthly count.

Message and data rates may apply.

STOP and HELP

Reply STOP to any MS Express TMS text to opt out. After you opt out we will send a one-time confirmation and then stop sending dispatch texts to that number. Reply HELP for help. You may also email jc@msloads.com or info@msloads.com, or call (877) 733-3966.

How long we keep the number

We keep a mobile number while the driver is in MS Express TMS and as needed for dispatch records, opt-out honoring, and ordinary business records. If a driver leaves or asks us to remove the number, we stop using it for new texts and keep only what we still need for records or legal reasons.
